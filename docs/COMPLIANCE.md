# Compliance & Cryptography

- **Gapless Site-Scoped Document Numbering**: Issued invoices, deposits, and credit notes assign gapless counters per `(company_id, series, year)` (`INV-2026-0001` or `INV-<site>-2026-0001`).
- **Immutability Triggers**: SQLite triggers reject mutations or deletions of issued documents and audit log entries.
- **Hash-Chained Audit Log**: Every mutation appends a SHA-256 chain record verified end-to-end by `GET /v1/audit/verify`.
- **Factur-X EN 16931 / PDF/A-3b**: Generated by Rust `auto-facturx` and verified for ICC/XMP/embedded CII XML metadata.
- **DGFiP FEC Export**: Balanced 18-column UTF/Latin-9 tab-separated ledger generated by `GET /v1/compliance/fec`.
- **Modern Cryptography**: `auto-auth` uses Argon2id (with constant-time Scrypt verification for legacy compatibility), ChaCha20-Poly1305 AEAD, Ed25519 EdDSA JWKS, HMAC-SHA256 tokens, RFC 6238 TOTP, and ES256 WebAuthn passkeys.
